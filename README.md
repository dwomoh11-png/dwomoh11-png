## Featured projects

### [Retail Customer Analytics](https://github.com/dwomoh11-png/retail-customer-analytics)

Analysed more than 541,000 retail transactions to explore completed-purchase revenue and historical customer behaviour. Developed customer-level RFM features, compared regression and classification models, and used K-means clustering to identify interpretable customer segments.

**Skills demonstrated:** Data cleaning, customer analytics, RFM analysis, regression, classification, clustering and business interpretation.

### [Used Car Price Analysis and Prediction](https://github.com/dwomoh11-png/used-car-price-prediction)

Analysed 4,009 used-car listings and compared Linear Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting models for estimating advertised prices. Evaluated performance on both original and log-transformed price scales and compared the models with a median-price baseline.

**Skills demonstrated:** Regression, preprocessing pipelines, target transformation, model comparison, feature importance and responsible model evaluation.

### [Banknote Authentication Analysis](https://github.com/dwomoh11-png/banknote-authentication-analysis)

Built and evaluated a Decision Tree classifier using four image-derived banknote features. Achieved 97.78% accuracy and 0.993 ROC-AUC on a stratified holdout set, then used K-means clustering to explore descriptive groups separately from the supervised class labels.

**Skills demonstrated:** Binary classification, confusion-matrix analysis, sensitivity, specificity, ROC-AUC, Decision Trees and K-means clustering.
